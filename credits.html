Behind the patch.

Maximalist is a home for student experiments in sound, image, and interaction. It collects final projects from Art 103 so people can see the work and open the patches behind it.

A website that thinks in patches.

Object boxes, inlets, outlets, and connected paths borrow their language from Max. A few short cables connect the title to the first row of artworks. Their styles represent audio, visual, interactive, and other work; they do not run or transmit the artwork.
