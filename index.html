?What is Max?

Max is a visual programming app made by Cycling ’74. Instead of typing code, you put small boxes called objects on a blank canvas and connect them with lines called patch cords. Each object does one small job, and the cords pass information from one to the next.

adc~listens to a mic
peakamp~measures how loud it is
jit.gl.gridshapedraws a shape that grows with the sound

Artists and musicians use it to build their own instruments, interactive installations and live visuals. A finished Max file is called a patch, which is why this whole site looks like one. Everything below was made this way by students at SJSU.
